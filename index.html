TAKE YOUR TIME

A little look around.

Drag anywhere in the room to look. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk; on a phone, touch and drag the lower-left part of the room to walk; the floating stick follows your starting point. Drag the right side to look around, then tap an object or its name to explore. Lift your thumb to stop. Open Room menu for books, lighting, music, and comfort settings. On desktop, choose Walk for mouse look and press Esc to release it.

The five numbered views take you to the room, workbench, bounty gallery, drone lab, and reading display. Point at an object to reveal its name and action. Tap it or press E to inspect it. Posters, drones, hardware, artwork, books, and everyday desk objects each have a closer view. Press Esc or Back to room to return to where you were. Tap a screen to use it. The Devices menu also lets you power each phone, tablet, and monitor on or off and change its screen. Choose Sit & use screen to sit down at the selected screen. Ubuntu connects to the crew workstation with its access password; the local Files, Editor, and Terminal apps are available to every visitor. Auto details balances sharpness with measured frame pacing. Choose Light for a smaller rendering budget or Detailed for more detail.

Tap a book to pick it up. Use arrows or swipe to turn its pages, bookmark a passage, or add private notes. Contemporary titles have companion pages. The archival Myanmar novel မောင်ရင်မောင် မမယ်မ and the original studio journal are fully readable. Import your own PDF, .txt, or .md manuscript to read it here. The lo-fi button mutes the original studio soundtrack.

to make things.

Open a file. Write a small program. Leave a note for your next visit. Your work stays in this browser.

Backbenchers Studio shell
Real files. Real Python. In your browser.
Type help for commands, or python hello.py to begin.

Code. Play. Flight.

An Ubuntu-inspired workspace made for the Backbenchers studio. The terminal runs supported shell commands and real Python through Pyodide.

The Ubuntu app connects to a real Ubuntu 24.04 LTS crew workstation with Xfce, a Linux terminal, applications, and persistent server files. A crew password is required. The separate local apps use your browser’s storage and Python runtime; they do not access Ubuntu files. Python downloads only when you first use the local workspace tools.

Small text files inside /home/studio persist in this browser (up to 2 MB total; 500 KB per file). Use Download for a copy you can keep. Stop restarts a busy runtime using your last completed save.
