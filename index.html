I’m Thomas D. Lynn, also known as Thiha Lynn — an independent software engineer, creative builder, and founder of Backbenchers Studio, with roots in Myanmar.

I work across games, web platforms, mobile apps, and creative tools. Practical software, people’s stories, and a little pirate spirit all have a place here.

This virtual studio is my solo project, with a wall celebrating the Backbenchers crew. The work displayed here includes collaborations, credited in the linked project stories.
