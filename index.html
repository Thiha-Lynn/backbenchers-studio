TAKE YOUR TIME

A little look around.

Drag anywhere in the room to look. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk; on a phone, use the left thumbstick and drag the room with your other thumb. On desktop, choose Walk for mouse look and press Esc to release it.

The four numbered views take you to the room, workbench, bounty gallery, and drone lab. Tap a screen or press E while looking at one. The Devices menu also lets you power each phone, tablet, and monitor on or off and change its screen. Choose Sit & use screen to sit down at the selected screen. Ubuntu connects to the crew workstation with its access password; the local Files, Editor, and Terminal apps are available to every visitor. Choose Light for lower resolution, or Detailed for crisper edges.

Open The crew to visit the three portfolios, or Selected work for project stories.

Code. Play. Flight.

An Ubuntu-inspired workspace made for the Backbenchers studio. The terminal runs supported shell commands and real Python through Pyodide.

The Ubuntu app connects to a real Ubuntu 24.04 LTS crew workstation with Xfce, a Linux terminal, applications, and persistent server files. A crew password is required. The separate local apps use your browser’s storage and Python runtime; they do not access Ubuntu files. Python downloads only when you first use the local workspace tools.

Small text files inside /home/studio persist in this browser (up to 2 MB total; 500 KB per file). Use Download for a copy you can keep. Stop restarts a busy runtime using your last completed save.
