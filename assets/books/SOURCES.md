Khet Zaw titles were cross-checked against [Pann Satt Lann](https://www.pannsattlann.com/author-tag/khat-zaw/), 4NIX, and My Yangon. Edition artwork can differ. Source resolution varies; the scene imports jackets up to 1024 px with mipmaps and anisotropic filtering, while the reading shelf uses the source files. Photographed editions use UV windows on the scene materials to isolate the jacket without changing the source image.

The compact computer is an original low-polygon study informed by [Apple's Mac mini reference](https://www.apple.com/mac-mini/specs/), not a downloaded Apple model. It is slightly enlarged for readability in the scene. Release 1.5 adds the requested Apple logo as a small inlay, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_logo_black.svg); Apple retains its trademark.

Additional covers: `freedom.jpg`, `letters.jpg`, and `hope.jpg` are the official Penguin jacket images for ISBNs 9780141039497, 9780141039534, and 9781846041433. `finding.jpg` is the Penguin Random House cover for ISBN 9780143037118. `hidden.jpg`, `china-india.jpg`, `river.jpg`, `modern.jpg`, and `peacemaker.jpg` are from [Thant Myint-U’s official book collection](https://www.thantmyintu.com/books). Source resolution varies, and no details or lettering have been invented through generative upscaling. Each book also has an original, title-specific spine label; these are studio-designed labels, not reproductions of the publisher's spine.

Reading-shelf WebP previews are compressed derivatives (up to 720 × 1080); the original downloaded covers remain beside them. No generative redraws were used for publisher covers. The new framed portrait is an original AI-generated faceless pencil interpretation; its generation prompt is saved in `assets/aung-san-suu-kyi-pencil-prompt.txt`. `assets/egunion-launcher.png` comes from the local EGUnionDecember project's configured Android application icon, `Assets/MFPS/Content/app launcher.png`. The phone itself is original procedural geometry and does not import gameplay systems.

Release 1.5: `assets/studio-nature-triptych.png` is original AI-generated artwork for the jasmine, river, and flight wall studies. Its full built-in generation prompt is stored beside it. Publisher jackets remain original reference artwork; their geometry and presentation are upgraded without rewriting their text.
